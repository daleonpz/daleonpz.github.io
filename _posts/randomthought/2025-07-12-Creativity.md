I think creativity is an iterative process that could begin with little to no knowledge of a subject. 

Everything starts with an interest in something, and from there, you explore and ask questions.

Let's say you want to design a new type of chair, but you don't know where to start. Well, you could just get a chair and **try** to understand why it was designed the way it was, why it's made of wood. Then you could ask if it's comfortable and why you find it comfortable.

I emphasize **trying**, because when you try to understand something, you'll start asking questions about the subject and also about your own understanding and assumptions.

An assumption could be "A chair has always 4 legs", but then you ask "wait... does a chair really need to have 4 legs?" 

Or a challenge to own understanding could be, "why if the chair has 4 legs, it keeps shaking, could it be that the floor is uneven or that the chair is not well designed?"

You see... Creativity is a process.
